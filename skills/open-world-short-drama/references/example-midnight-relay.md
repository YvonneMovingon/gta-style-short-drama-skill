# Midnight Relay · 原创开放世界短剧示例

**Logline:** A courier delivers a tape to a shuttered station, then races a storm to get its last broadcast back on the air.

**Format:** 10 missions, 12 seconds each, 16:9. Third-person camera, practical light, humid coastal nights. English dialogue and optional narration. Dark radio dial and the tape's orange tab serve as repeatable visual cues. Add mission titles in editing if needed.

**World and recurring assets**

- **C01 Ren**, night courier, early 30s, short black curls, warm brown skin, mustard rain jacket, dark trousers, slate crossbody bag. Speaks in short, dry observations.
- **C02 Sol**, former station technician, early 40s, cropped silver hair, olive skin, teal work shirt over a white T-shirt, round glasses. Speaks gently and precisely.
- **P01 tape**: transparent compact cassette with one orange tab. Ren carries it until M05, Sol loads it in M09. It never changes color or duplicates.
- **V01 van**: weathered white delivery van with one teal stripe, unchanged throughout its appearances.
- **W01 Tern Harbor**: lower waterfront, shuttered station, and elevated lighthouse studio linked by a service road. Purple dusk becomes rainy blue through M09, then clears into dawn. White circular location badges can be added as original overlays.

**Spine:** Tape pickup → unexpected locked door → find Sol → hear the recorded clue → retrieve the station's field recorder → van route closes → cross the footbridge → enter the lighthouse → broadcast the tape → see the harbor respond.
